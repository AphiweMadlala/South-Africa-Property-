# Surface brief — website (home, residence index, residence pages)

Scope: the official South Africa Property website. Mode: Experience for the home and residence pages (the homes lead), Operate for the residence index (browse and filter).

Audience and job: people who saw a home on @southafrica.property, mostly on phones, wanting to see it properly, place it, know if it can be bought, and enquire by email or WhatsApp; agents and owners wanting a feature.

Constraints: every fact comes from the extracted posts; status per documentation/content-model.md; no invented contacts; owner's brief bans (no navy/gold, no banned fonts or copy, no centred hero with two buttons, no search box over the hero, no pill overload, no glass or gradient mesh).

Memorable moment: opening a residence — one uninterrupted run of its photography with the facts set like a printed feature.

Unresolved: contact email and WhatsApp number (owner to supply); real photography (waiting on extraction).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A classic editorial showcase: each home presented as a magazine feature and indexed by place. It refuses the portal pattern (search bar and card grid above the fold) and the dark "luxury" template.

OWN-WORLD: White and pale fynbos-stone grounds, near-black ink, one deep fynbos-green accent. Libre Caslon Display for names and headlines; Archivo (width axis) for places, prices, specs, navigation and metadata. Square corners, no shadows, hairlines only in data tables. Photography edge to edge.

STORY: The visitor meets one exceptional home first, sees the national reach by place, browses by place and status, opens a home to its full gallery and facts, then enquires.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A slim header (wordmark left, four links right). A full-bleed cover photograph about 82vh tall. On desktop a caption sits bottom-left over the image: place in tracked caps, the home's name in Caslon up to 5.5rem, its status, and one "View the residence" link. On mobile the caption sits below the image. No second button.

FORM: Classic editorial, the canon the owner chose (standing exit, outside the ranked list). Seed key 30c0f01a.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
