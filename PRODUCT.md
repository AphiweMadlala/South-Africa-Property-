# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated (owner, 2026-09-26): plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript generated by a Node
build script from the extracted Instagram data. No framework. Published from
`docs/` on `main` with GitHub Pages under the project path
`/South-Africa-Property-/`. Chosen for speed, nothing to maintain, and a direct fit
with GitHub Pages.

## Users

- **Primary:** people interested in high-end South African homes. They include
  prospective buyers and people who follow residential architecture. They mostly
  arrive from the Instagram account, usually on a phone. They want to see each home
  properly, understand where it is and what it offers, know whether it can be
  bought, and make contact.
- **Also:** estate agents, agencies, developers and owners who want a home featured
  (the account runs paid listing features).

## Product Purpose

The official website of South Africa Property (@southafrica.property). The account
showcases high-end residential properties in and around South Africa. The site
turns its Instagram archive into a browsable national collection: every featured
residence with its photography, place, facts and credits. It separates homes that
are for sale now from homes shown editorially. Success: visitors explore homes by
place and type, understand what is available, and enquire by email or WhatsApp.

## Positioning

A curated national index of South African residences, built from the account's
own feature archive. It is not a property portal and not an agency site. Every fact
links back to the post it came from, and availability is stated only when there is
evidence for it.

## Operating Context

- Content starts as Instagram posts (carousels, reels, single images with
  captions). The website is generated from an extraction of those posts (Apify,
  run by a GitHub workflow) and is refreshed by running the extraction again.
- Business model (owner, 2026-09-26): a mix of editorial features, paid listing
  features for agents and owners, and listings the account represents. Which
  category each post belongs to is not yet known.
- Enquiries (owner, 2026-09-26): by email or WhatsApp to South Africa Property.
  **The address and number have not been provided yet and must not be invented.**

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site with no server. Index filters run client-side, and their state lives
  in the URL.
- Content and status rules are in `documentation/content-model.md`:
  - "for sale" requires recent evidence and a named listing credit;
  - otherwise a home is shown as "availability to confirm" or "featured";
  - missing facts stay missing.
- Credits appear only as the captions state them. The site never implies that the
  brand employs, partners with or represents anyone it credits.
- Many photographs belong to photographers and agencies. Credits and links to
  source posts are kept.
- Undecided: contact email and WhatsApp number; custom domain.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **South Africa Property**. Instagram: **@southafrica.property**.
- Bio, from the owner's brief: "Showcasing the best high-end residential properties
  in and around South Africa."
- Visual register (owner's choice, 2026-09-26): **classic editorial**, the
  familiar high-end property showcase done straight and at full craft, with
  **Sotheby's International Realty** as the quality benchmark. The benchmark sets
  the level of craft (calm, spacious, photography-led, precise type and listing
  detail). It does not set the palette or typefaces, which the bans below
  exclude (e.g. navy and gold).
- Binding constraints from the owner's brief:
  - Concept: "Modern South African Residence Index": contemporary architecture
    journal + private residential catalogue + national property index + verified
    sales layer.
  - South African without tourism clichés, flag colours, safari motifs,
    faux-African patterns or generic luxury gold. Not navy-and-gold, black-and-gold
    or a beige interior template.
  - Not a real-estate portal, an Airbnb or Property24 clone, or a luxury
    marketplace template.
  - Fonts to avoid: Inter, Roboto, Arial, Open Sans, Poppins, Montserrat, DM Sans,
    Space Grotesk, generic system sans, and component-library defaults.
  - Copy to avoid: "Luxury redefined", "Elevated living", "Find your dream home",
    "Where luxury meets lifestyle", "unparalleled luxury", "timeless elegance",
    "prestigious living", "elevated lifestyle". Location and architecture carry
    the substance.

## Evidence on Hand

- No posts, photographs, prices, places or people have been extracted yet.

  people, testimonials, statistics or company history.

## Product Principles

1. The homes are the product. Photography and place lead, and the interface
   recedes.
2. Every fact traces back to its post. What is missing stays missing.
3. Availability is a dated claim, never a default.
4. South Africa is the organising system: province, then city, then suburb or
   estate.
5. Credit precisely and generously.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Required by the owner's brief:

- full keyboard use, including the gallery lightbox (Escape and focus restoration);
- visible focus order;
- reduced-motion support;
- sufficient contrast;
- touch targets sized for phones, where most visitors are;
- no horizontal overflow from 375 to 1920 px.
