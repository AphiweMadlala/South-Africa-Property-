## Summary

The website is built, reviewed and tested. It has been verified on a labelled
design preview with synthetic content, and on a synthetic Instagram export run
through the whole pipeline.

It has not been built from the account's real posts yet. The Instagram archive
could not be extracted in this project's environment (see
[access log](research/00-access-log.md)), and the GitHub route still waits on
one setting: `APIFY_TOKEN` must be a GitHub **Actions** repository secret. Until
the first build from real posts, the live address shows the holding page.

Once the secret is added, three steps take the site live (README, "Going live
with the real archive"):

1. a free dry run;
2. the paid extraction, which also builds the site;
3. a merge into `main`, or nothing more if the run started on `main`.

## Business model

The owner states that the account runs a mix of three things:

- editorial features, where homes are shown for their architecture;
- paid listing features for agents and agencies;
- listings the account represents itself.

The site is built for all three:

- **Credits.** Each residence names the agent or agency the post credits
  ("Represented by …"). No partnership or employment is implied.
- **Status.** A home is "For sale" only when a post from the last 30 days
  offers it and names the agent. Older sale posts read "Offered for sale
  when featured, *month year* · availability to confirm". Homes without sale
  information read "Featured".
- **Enquiries.** The Enquire page and each residence's buttons carry email
  and WhatsApp once the owner supplies them. Until then they offer an
  Instagram message.

The extraction will show how the three appear in the captions, for example
"Listed by", "DM to be featured" or price lines. That split is recorded as a
data gap below until then.

## Design and review

- **Direction:** classic editorial, chosen by the owner, with Sotheby's
  International Realty as the craft benchmark.
- **Type:** Libre Caslon Display with Archivo, self-hosted.
- **Palette:** white and fynbos-stone grounds, near-black ink and one fynbos
  green.
- **Shapes and effects:** square corners, no shadows, hairlines for data only.

Full system: [DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md).

The design went through two internal inspection rounds, one detector run and
independent finish reviews:

1. The first review asked for new screenshots, because the capture method had
   left lazy-loaded images blank. It also flagged a real defect: the empty
   state showed under the results.
2. The full review asked for eight fixes:
   - the story block;
   - cover contrast over bright photographs;
   - duplicated homes on the home page;
   - the section order;
   - the Enquire layout;
   - the wide feature's link;
   - About, Enquire and Places composition;
   - the phone status filter and the card baselines.

   All eight were applied, and the cover title now measures at least 4.2:1
   over a near-white photograph.
3. A final verdict pass confirmed all eight fixes and found no regressions. It
   found one remaining blocker, present since the first build: on phones the
   cover's photo credit sat on the bare photograph (1.68:1). See "Review
   verdict" below.

## Data gaps

Nothing below has been filled in. Where a value is unknown, the site omits it.

**Account**
- Follower, following and post counts; account category; profile links;
  public contact details; posting history and cadence.
- The live bio, which is taken from the owner's brief and not yet checked
  against the profile.

**Content (every residence)**
- Posts, captions, photographs, reels and their dates.
- Residence names, places (province, city, suburb or estate), prices, bedroom,
  bathroom and garage counts, erf and floor sizes, and property types.
- Sale status and its date for every home, including which homes are for sale
  now.
- The people and firms credited: agents and agencies, architects, interior and
  landscape designers, developers, builders, photographers.

**Business**
- How editorial features, paid listing features and the account's own
  listings split across the archive, and how each is worded in captions.
- Whether and how the account charges for features.
- Which listings the account represents directly.

**Enquiries**
- The enquiry email address and WhatsApp number. The owner chose these
  channels but has not supplied them; `site/config.json` keeps both `null`.

**Imagery**
- Photography rights beyond what each post credits. The site states that
  photographs remain their owners' property and credits them as published.
- Whether any photograph carries a watermark. Watermarks are kept as
  published.

**Research not yet possible**
- The recurring properties, places, captions and calls to action. These are
  produced by `research/02-archive-analysis.md` from the extraction.
- Checking listings against portals (Property24, Private Property). The
  portals are unreachable from this environment, so no availability is
  claimed beyond what posts state, with their dates.

**Platform**
- A custom domain. A project site cannot serve its own `robots.txt` or
  sitemap at the domain root.

## Review verdict

**Verdict: ship, after one fix measured in place of a further review round.**

- **Last review.** The final verdict pass returned **fix** with a single
  blocking item: the phone cover credit. All eight earlier fixes were
  confirmed, with no regressions.
- **The fix.** On phones the credit is now the photograph's caption line, on
  the night panel directly below the image. It measures 8.3:1, and does not
  depend on the photograph behind it.
- **Checks after the fix.** QA passes 198 of 198 and the tests pass 68 of 68.
  All fourteen review captures were retaken from the final build with no
  overflow or errors.
- **Not repeated.** The review loop's two-round limit was reached, so the fix
  was verified by measurement rather than by another reviewer round.

The first build from real posts still needs its own pass: QA, a check of the
residence stories against their captions, and a look at the cover on the
actual lead photograph.
